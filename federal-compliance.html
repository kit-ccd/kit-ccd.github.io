Federal Compliance & Cloud Advisory

Navigating federal security mandates requires bridging the gap between rigorous compliance frameworks and modern cloud architectures. Civilian Cyber Defense provides specialized advisory services designed to help small-to-midsize defense contractors, government suppliers, and tech firms establish secure baselines and accelerate authorization.

Core Expertise & Frameworks

  • NIST SP 800-53 (Rev 5): Establishing and mapping technical and operational control baselines across multi-cloud and hybrid environments.
  • DISA STIGs: Implementing granular configuration standards and hardening infrastructure, containers, and cloud control planes.
  • FedRAMP & GovCloud Readiness: Assisting teams with boundary definition, metadata hygiene, and continuous monitoring posture preparation before going in front of Authorizing Officials.
  • Infrastructure as Code (IaC) & CI/CD Security: Embedding automated compliance checks early into development pipelines to prevent misconfigurations.

Our Approach

We work directly with engineering and leadership teams to replace retrospective, manual audits with continuous, automated posture management. Whether you are preparing for an upcoming ATO (Authority to Operate) or scaling your security posture to meet Department of Defense requirements, we provide the strategic roadmap to get you there.
